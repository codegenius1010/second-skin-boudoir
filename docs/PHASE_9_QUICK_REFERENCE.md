## 📈 TRAFFIC FORECAST METHODOLOGY

### How Ranking Leads to Traffic

```
1 Page 1 ranking (avg position 5) = ~150-200 monthly visitors
2-5 Page 1 rankings (position 10-15) = ~50-100 visitors each
6-15 Page 1 rankings (position 15-20) = ~30-50 visitors each
```

**Example**:
- 15 Page 1 keywords × 100 avg visitors = 1,500 organic visitors/month
- Current: ~100 organic visitors (1-2 rankings)

## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. ❌ Ignoring Search Console alerts
   - Fix crawl errors immediately
   
2. ❌ Writing new content without keyword research
   - Always research keywords before writing

3. ❌ Ignoring user behavior signals
   - Monitor bounce rate, time on page
   - If high bounce rate, improve content

4. ❌ Neglecting mobile experience
   - Test on actual mobile devices
   - Use Google Mobile-Friendly Test

5. ❌ Not updating old content
   - Refresh top performers every 6 months
   - Add new information, update dates

6. ❌ Focusing only on rankings
   - Rankings don't matter without conversions
   - Track actual business results

7. ❌ Expecting overnight results
   - SEO takes 3-6 months for real traction
   - Consistent effort compounds over time

## 📞 SUPPORT & TROUBLESHOOTING

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Q: Why are we not ranking yet?**
A: New sites typically take 3-6 months. Check:
- Search Console shows pages indexed
- No manual actions or crawl errors
- Internal links point to target pages

**Q: Form submissions aren't increasing**
A: May need marketing efforts (PPC, social). Check:
- Form is visible on key pages
- CTAs are clear and prominent
- Trust signals effective

**Q: Mobile traffic is low**
A: Check Google Mobile-Friendly Test:
- All elements responsive
- Text readable without zoom
- Buttons are 48px minimum
